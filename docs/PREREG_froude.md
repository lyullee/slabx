# Pre-registration: replacing the fixed reference height in EQ 35a

**Registered 2026-08-16, before any comparison was run.**
Do not edit this file after results are known. Results go in `RESULTS`
below, appended.

---

## Background

SLAB's vertical entrainment velocity (EQ 35a, SLAB.FOR L2872) is

    w = (urf / uah) * aa * kappa * u* * g(h/H) / phi

where `urf = uafn(hrf, ...)` and `hrf = 4.0` (SLAB.FOR L466). That is an
**absolute length inside a similarity closure**. Scaling a release by 1000 in
length, with velocity as sqrt(s) and rate as s^2.5 — which density-Froude
similarity requires to leave concentration unchanged — changes it by 2.391.
Scaling `hrf` with the geometry brings that to 1.571.

The diagnosis is that the fixed height is the dominant cause. This test asks
whether removing it entirely closes the gap.

## What is being tested

**The diagnosis, not the prescription.** A published entrainment function
that carries no absolute length replaces the `urf/uah` factor. If the
diagnosis is right, the scale ratio moves toward 1. Whether the replacement
is *better* against field data is a different question and is not the
criterion here.

Two candidates, both taken as published, neither refitted:

**(N) Nielsen (1998), Risø-R-1030(EN)**

    u_e / e = 0.25 / (3.3 + Ri)
    e = (u*^3 + 0.1 w*^3)^(2/3)

**(R) Robins et al. (2001), Atmos. Environ. 35, 2243-2252**

    W_E / u* = 0.65 / (1 + 0.2 Ri*)        (Ri* < 15)

`Ri*` uses the cloud's own bulk Richardson number, `g' h / u*^2`, with
`g' = g (rho - rho_a) / rho_a`. Robins is the primary because it was measured
directly in a wind tunnel rather than inferred from a model.

## Predictions

| # | Prediction | Criterion |
|---|---|---|
| **P-F1** | Removing the fixed 4 m brings the Froude scale ratio toward unity | at s = 1000, **ratio < 1.3** (now 2.391; 1.571 with `hrf` scaled) |
| **P-F2** | The effect is confined to scale transfer; field statistics barely move | field MG change **< 5 %** on the ten LNG pool trials |
| **P-F3** | Any residual means a **second absolute scale** remains | if the ratio settles between 1.0 and 1.3, report its size and look for the second scale |
| **NC** | Negative control: at s = 1 there is nothing to change | ratio exactly 1.000 |

## What would falsify the diagnosis

If the ratio stays above 1.5 with the fixed height gone, the 4 m reference is
not the dominant cause and §5.2 of the paper must be rewritten.

## What must not happen

The coefficients (0.25, 3.3, 0.65, 0.2) are **not** to be adjusted to improve
any statistic. The project's own argument is that fitting coefficients to
field data manufactures the compensation it is trying to expose; doing it
here would contradict the paper from inside.

The new form is a variant. The default stays as Ermak wrote it.

**NC passes**: every variant returns exactly 1.000 at s = 1.

### P-F1 — FAILED

Criterion was a ratio inside [1/1.3, 1.3] at s = 1000. Both closures give
about **1.5**, which is barely better than merely scaling the fixed height
(1.569) and nowhere near unity.

**The fixed 4 m is not the dominant cause.** It accounts for roughly half of
the departure in log terms; removing it entirely rather than scaling it buys
almost nothing more.

That two independently published closures — one fitted to four canonical
flows, one measured in a wind tunnel — land within 1 % of each other (1.502
against 1.488) says the residual is a property of **the rest of the model**,
not of which entrainment function is chosen.

### P-F3 — triggered, second scale not yet found

The residual is 1.5, above the 1.0-1.3 band anticipated. Two candidates were
tested and neither is it:

| candidate | scaled with s | effect |
|---|---|---|
| meander reference times (`tau_min` 10 s, `t_ref_avg` 900 s) | yes | **none** (1.502 → 1.502) |
| the fixed 2 m in EQ 36a (`h_horiz_ref`) | yes | **none** (2.387 → 2.387) |

Both were externalised as coefficients to run the test and are kept.
The second absolute scale is elsewhere; the source-region treatment and the
roughness-dependent profile remain untested.

### P-F2 — FAILED, and this is the important result

Ten LNG pool trials, distance to LFL:

| closure | FAC2 | MG | VG | FB | NMSE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| slab (as written) | 0.90 | 1.252 | 1.225 | +0.117 | 0.121 |
| **Robins** | **1.00** | **0.997** | **1.089** | −0.049 | **0.073** |
| Nielsen | 0.00 | 2.975 | 3.823 | +1.003 | 1.764 |

Criterion was a field MG change under 5 %. Robins moves it **20 %** and
Nielsen **138 %**. Failed.

**Robins is better on every aggregate measure than both the original and
Phast 8.4** (FAC2 0.90, MG 0.996, NMSE 0.204). Every point inside a factor
of two. It is the best field result this project has produced.

**It must not be adopted.**

The mechanism it was introduced to fix — Froude similarity — **was not
fixed**. P-F1 failed. What improved is the aggregate, and the paper's whole
argument is that an aggregate improving while the mechanism stays broken is
the signature of compensation, not of correction.

Adopting it would mean doing, inside the paper, the thing the paper is
about. The pre-registration exists for exactly this moment: the predictions
were written down before the numbers were known, and the numbers are not
allowed to rewrite them afterwards.

This is the fourth time in the project an aggregate statistic pointed the
opposite way from the mechanism test (the others: canonical `C_mu`, the
0.01 m roughness, the height closure). It is the first time it happened to a
change the authors expected to succeed.

### Dimensional check on Nielsen

The form was supplied as ``e = (u*^3 + 0.1 w*^3)^(2/3)``. That gives `e` the
dimensions of velocity squared, so `u_e / e` is an inverse velocity, and
under Froude scaling the entrainment velocity would go as `s` where the
similarity requires `sqrt(s)`. The first run showed exactly that — a ratio of
0.148 at s = 1000, diverging downward. With the exponent 1/3 the scaling is
correct and the ratio is 1.488. Recorded because a dimensional check settled
it without needing the source.

## ADDENDUM — decomposition, second dataset, and two transcription errors

*(2026-08-16, after review. Predictions above still unedited.)*

The first write-up rejected Robins on the aggregate alone, which is the
procedure this project argues against. Corrected below.

### Two errors in the supplied Nielsen form

Both found by internal consistency, not by fitting.

| # | supplied | correct | how it was found |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `e = (u*^3 + 0.1 w*^3)^(2/3)` | **`^(1/3)`** | `^(2/3)` gives `e` the dimensions of velocity squared, so `u_e/e` is an inverse velocity and Froude scaling gives `u_e ~ s` where `sqrt(s)` is required |
| 2 | `u_e/e = 0.25/(3.3 + Ri)` | **`2.5/(3.3 + Ri)`** | at Ri = 0 in neutral air `e = u*`, so `u_e/u*` must equal Nielsen's own passive limit λ₁ = 0.75. 0.25/3.3 = 0.076, a factor ten below it; 2.5/3.3 = 0.758 |

A third error was ours, not the source's: `w*` was implemented as the
cloud's own buoyancy `(g' h u*)^(1/3)`. Nielsen's `w*` is the **atmospheric**
convective scale from the surface heat flux, and is zero in neutral or stable
air. A dense cloud is stably stratified and does not convect; the
substitution made its stratification act as a source of mixing energy, giving
`w*` = 0.87 against `u*` = 0.065 on Burro 8. Now taken from `1/L` of the
ambient.

With all three corrected, Nielsen behaves: FAC2 0.30 → **0.90**, MG 2.144 →
**1.193**.

### Three grounds for rejection, none of them the aggregate

**1. The effect is not where the mechanism puts it.** Robins is a friction
velocity closure, so its effect should track `u*` — strongest in strong wind
and neutral stratification. The wind correlation is +0.31, weak. The
stability correlation is **−0.86**: the change acts almost entirely on the
three unstable trials and does essentially nothing (1.02-1.06) on the six
neutral and one stable ones.

Those three unstable trials are exactly where the original was worst
(147 against 190, 129 against 206, 89 against 245). **The change acts where
the model was already wrong, not where its own mechanism says it should.**
That is the same shape as the canonical `C_mu` case in §4.2 — an effect
appearing where it was not predicted.

**2. The two published datasets judge it oppositely.** Robins takes the
distance comparison to FAC2 1.00 and MG 0.997, and takes the concentration
comparison from FAC2 0.67 down to **0.50**. Same model change, same four
trials, opposite verdicts.

This is the **third** instance of the dataset contradiction, after the
roughness recommendation and the implied LFL distances. It is now shown for
a model change as well as for a parameter choice, which is a stronger
statement than either alone.

**3. The mechanism it was introduced to fix was not fixed.** P-F1 failed:
1.502 against the registered 1.3, barely better than scaling the fixed
height (1.569).

### What this does not say

Robins is not shown to be wrong. It was measured directly in a wind tunnel
and may well be a better closure. What is shown is that **this comparison
cannot establish that**, because the evidence points three different ways
depending on which dataset and which decomposition is used — which is the
paper's subject rather than an obstacle to it.

The default stays as Ermak wrote it. Both closures remain available as
variants.
